# ESTATESTAR — design system

Updated 2026-10-18. Visual authority: the original estatestar.de identity, reconstructed in ../estatestar-preview. The preceding beige/Helvetica design study is superseded.

## Brand commitments

- Original multicolored ESTATESTAR logo. No added decorative asterisk or invented brand symbol.
- Michroma for chapter titles, navigation and the italic original claim; Eurostile for body, captions and controls.
- White #FFFFFF, black #020202, original orange #EE683C. No warm beige, generic oversized grotesk or serif accent word.
- Genuine architecture images and films are the main visual content. Original mission glass-facade background. Original chapter logic: white PHOTO, black FILM, white 360, full video DIGITAL LAB, black TEAM, white KONTAKT.
- No invented testimonials, customer lists, project details or performance statistics. AI images remain clearly labelled as original source photographs, not AI results.

## Homepage direction

Experience mode: the work leads. A full-screen film window with restrained original claim; a white mission plane over the original blue facade; a six-image photographic contact sheet; a large cinematic frame with a direct film index; a clear tour entry; a second full-screen Digital Lab film; practical AI concept disclosure; portraits and direct contact.

The steady logo and chapter navigation, together with native scroll snap, connect the changing images. No scroll interception, artificial delay, custom cursor, decorative orbit or chapter numbering. Mobile rearranges content without hiding essential material; long chapters stay freely scrollable.

## Interaction and accessibility

Native dialogs for film viewing and biographies, Escape and focus return; real photo gallery links; filter selection; six genuine tour targets; labelled mail draft form. Visible keyboard focus and themed selection. Image hover is restrained and disabled under reduced motion. Ambient videos only play in their visible chapter and pause for dialogs, inactive windows or explicit user choice. Reduced motion disables video autoplay and scroll snap.
