# ESTATESTAR — design system

Updated 2026-10-18 after David’s request for a standards-based critique and correction of Version 03. The original estatestar.de identity is the reference; earlier layout decisions are revisable, not brand requirements.

## Brand and hierarchy

Original multicolored ESTATESTAR logo, Michroma, Eurostile, white #FFFFFF, black #020202 and orange #EE683C. No added decorative asterisk. Michroma carries the original italic claim and compact chapter headings. Eurostile carries navigation, body copy, controls and captions. Small accent text uses #B74720 against white (5.34:1), while original orange remains the decorative accent and button background with black text.

Genuine architecture photographs and films are the main visual content. Preserve white PHOTO, black FILM, white 360°, immersive DIGITAL LAB, black TEAM and white KONTAKT. No invented testimonials, customer lists, project details or performance statistics. AI remains a proposed service chapter without pretend before-and-after examples.

## Composition

- Hero: one quiet, title-free architecture shot, the original claim, a plain service description and a clear showreel action. The complete original showreel opens on demand.
- Mission: original glass-facade image beside readable text on desktop, beneath it on mobile.
- Photography: compact heading and category controls above two generous columns, one on mobile. Captions below images. Full galleries preserve natural image proportions, without arbitrary alternating spans or crops.
- Film: large 16:9 feature and six actual film previews; a two-column index below the feature on mobile.
- Tours: one clear real preview and a labelled selector for six genuine destinations.
- Digital Lab: the original full-screen film chapter. AI: separate honest concept chapter, with three directly named proposals and no misleading image demonstration.
- Team and contact: coherent headings, readable biographies, direct email/telephone and an optional email-draft form.

Desktop navigation exposes main services. Contact stays visible on mobile. The menu includes all chapters, language and scroll-snap preference. Avoid competing decorative navigation, giant repeated title screens, microtype and arbitrary spacing.

## Interaction and accessibility

Native chapter scroll snap on desktop/mobile; long chapters remain freely scrollable. Snap can be switched off from menu or footer. Reduced-motion settings disable autoplay and snap. Ambient video pauses offscreen, during dialogs, in background tabs or by user choice. The header has a strong dark gradient over moving media.

Native dialogs support Escape and focus return. Choosing a chapter from the menu transfers focus to that chapter’s heading. Gallery return links retain accessible labels when their visible text is hidden. Filter selection, clear focus states and comfortable touch targets are retained. Contact creates a mailto draft, never sends automatically.

This is a practical design and browser review, not a claim of complete WCAG conformance. W3C AA target-size minimum is 24 CSS px subject to its exceptions; 44 px is our comfort goal for primary controls.

Two independent reviews identified the oversized repeated headings, undersized work previews, tiny controls and competing video titles. A second visual pass identified header contrast over bright footage, subsequently corrected. No arbitrary design score was used.
