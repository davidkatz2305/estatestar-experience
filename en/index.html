01 / STUDIOARCHITECTURE. PERSPECTIVE. EMOTION.

Spaces are more
than square metres.

They have character.
We make it visible.

We are ESTATESTAR. A photo, film and 360° studio specialising in architecture. We bring individual properties and large portfolios to life — from the ground, from the air and in virtual spaces.

From the first perspective to the final detail. With helicopter, drone, camera and 3D scanner. Post-production takes place in our studio and Digital Lab in Berlin.
